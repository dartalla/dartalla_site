# Product

<!-- impeccable:product-schema 1 -->

## Platform

web

## Stack

Static HTML, CSS and JavaScript (Bootstrap 5.3 Collapse only, Motion and Lenis vendored locally). Deployed on Vercel from the `main` branch. No framework migration without a request.

## Users

MEIs, autonomous professionals and small businesses in Brazil who are not comfortable with technology, even with AI present in everything. They arrive wary and want to understand, in plain Portuguese, who Dartalla is and whether its product fits their business. Site goal: build trust in the company, send visitors to the product (https://dartalla.app) or to a WhatsApp conversation with the team.

## Product Purpose

This is the company site of Dartalla (Dartalla Tecnologia LTDA, CNPJ 54.322.477/0001-12), not the product site. Dartalla creates technology solutions for people and small businesses, mainly MEIs and autonomous professionals. Dartalla One is the current product (a multi-segment suite covering financial, fiscal and operational management) and appears here as one section, with real screenshots and a link to its own landing page.

## Positioning

Simple technology for people who undertake. Close and calm rather than technical or loud. The company explains itself without jargon; the product is shown as evidence, not as the whole identity.

## Operating Context

Brazilian market, Portuguese (pt-BR). Contact is by WhatsApp. Legal documents (terms, privacy) are generated from the Core App source by `scripts/sync-legal-documents.mjs`.

## Capabilities and Constraints

- Public claims about the company or the product need confirmed evidence. Never invent customers, quotes, metrics, certifications, guarantees, other products or commercial terms.
- Only the product named Dartalla One may be cited, at https://dartalla.app. Other Dartalla products are not public.
- Product screenshots must be real captures of Dartalla One with safe demonstration data (`scripts/demo_gateway.py`), recaptured when the interface changes.
- WhatsApp clicks are contact intentions only: no persisted leads, no contact data in analytics.
- Content and WhatsApp links must work without JavaScript; motion must respect reduced-motion.

## Brand Commitments

Lago brand, white-first: blue `#136A9B`, teal companion `#2BA89A` for data only, Inter Tight headings and Inter text. Company logo is the symbol plus "Dartalla" (no "One" badge); the "One" badge belongs to the product logo. Source of truth: the Dartalla One design system artifact. No violet, no sparkles, no neon gradients.

## Evidence on Hand

Legal entity and CNPJ, the WhatsApp contact, the live product at https://dartalla.app, and four demonstration screenshots. No customer logos, testimonials or usage numbers are on hand. Facts about the company mission come from the owner's brief of 2026-11-25 and are labeled as owner-confirmed.

## Product Principles

1. Explain without jargon: if a visitor needs technical knowledge to understand a sentence, rewrite it.
2. The company comes first, the product proves it.
3. Say only what can be shown.
4. Offer a human next step (WhatsApp) beside the product link.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

Plain language for non-technical readers, large touch targets, visible keyboard focus, contrast at WCAG AA or better, no content hidden behind animation, reduced-motion respected.
